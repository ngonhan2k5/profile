TM Forum Trustworthy AI Hackathon 2026

Finalist · top 6  Challenge: Agentic Assurance, The Quest for Proof. Dallas, October 4–7, 2026, with AWS, NVIDIA, ServiceNow, Accenture and Dell as partners.

  • Problem: Three AI agents on AWS Bedrock AgentCore resolve network faults together. How can an auditor prove they followed policy?
  • What we built: A control layer with three controls, each judged against a threshold frozen before the run: event traceability, drift and a token cap. A control test tool answers "was control N satisfied for this run?" with PASS, BREACH or NO EVIDENCE, plus the records behind the verdict.
  • My role: Platform lead. I built the inline token-spend guard in our own agent, which also hands decisions to a person when it shouldn't decide alone.
  • Approach: Policy decisions stay outside the execution path: a register, then a stateless evaluator, then an evidence ledger, then a query tool. We tested the guardrails with adversarial inputs generated by Nemotron and published an honest list of gaps.
